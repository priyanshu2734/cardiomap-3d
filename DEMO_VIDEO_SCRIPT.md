## 30-second addition: Physics vs data

Use this immediately after the flow-curve portion, while the selected vessel is LAD (or switch to LCX/RCA to demonstrate the weak-vessel note).

| Time | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| +0:00-0:10 | Physics curve: move the narrowing and toggle Rest/Exercise; current dot follows the live setting. | “The curve is a simplified physics model. It asks what this artery would do in theory as the diameter gets narrower; these thresholds are model outputs, not clinical cut-offs.” |
| +0:10-0:22 | Scroll to the cohort panel; show Narrowed versus Not narrowed rows and one Wilson interval. | “The data panel asks a different question: what was actually recorded in these 303 referred patients. It shows counts, rates and Wilson intervals, not a prediction for your heart.” |
| +0:22-0:30 | Show the caveat/weak-vessel note; do not alter the narrowing. | “It is descriptive only—one referred cohort, no external validation, no causal claim. LCX and RCA estimates are weaker, and this panel never changes the simulation.” |

**30-second safety line to keep visible:** “Simplified physics model, not patient-specific or clinical” and “Descriptive rates in 303 referred patients; not a validation of the physics model and not a prediction for your heart”.
